# NIMA

> NIMA is a portable, consumer-use gluten detection sensor for people living with celiac disease and gluten sensitivity. Load a pea-sized food sample into a single-use capsule, insert it into the NIMA Sensor, and get a clear result in about three minutes — detection down to 10 ppm, the last check before the first bite. Browse the catalog, read product data, and check out at https://nimanow.com.

Relaunched and rebuilt from the ground up in 2025 with investment from RA Capital Management, NIMA pairs a next-generation gluten detection sensor with newly engineered single-use test capsules and the NIMA Now™ app. NIMA is a screening tool, not a medical device — it adds one more layer of information to gluten-free living that already takes judgment, experience, and care.

## Responsible Use & Accuracy

NIMA is not a guarantee; it is a tool — for the moment after you've read the label, asked the kitchen, and made your best assessment, and uncertainty remains.

- A screening tool, not a diagnostic or medical device.
- Tests only the pea-sized sample placed in the capsule, not the entire dish.
- A "no gluten found" result does not guarantee a food is completely gluten-free.
- Results depend on sampling technique, food type, and context; not indicated for fermented or hydrolyzed foods.
- Does not replace allergen labeling, restaurant communication, dietary counseling, or clinical care.
